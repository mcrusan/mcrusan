# Marc F. Crusan — Founder & Chief Systems Architect, Crusan & Co.

I am a systems architect, bare-metal C developer, and former U.S. Army Staff Sergeant (`11B3G`) with more than three decades of hands-on experience across enterprise networking, embedded silicon, operating systems engineering, and large-scale operational leadership. Over the course of my career across military, government, and private-sector environments, I have built, secured, and modernized some of the most geographically dispersed and mission-critical networks in the world.

Today, I lead **Crusan & Co.**, where my family and I build **[AxisOS™](https://axisos.pro)** (*U.S. Trademark Application Serial No. `99878469`*)—a deterministic, RAM-resident operating system (`axisos-kernel.img`), freestanding C99 direct-syscall userland (`svc #0`), and multi-agent embedded hardware ecosystem built on three permanent pillars: **HONEST · AUDITABLE · OWNED**.

---

## Technical Foundation

My bench foundation spans low-level and enterprise stacks alike: freestanding C99, C/C++, ARM64 (`AArch64` & `ARMv6`/`RP2350`) bare-metal assembly and register programming, Java, Visual Basic, PHP, Perl, SQL, DNS, SMTP, POSIX/UNIX internals, and Windows internals—paired with decades of hands-on work in routers, switches, firewalls, hardware diagnostics, and microcomputer repair. That breadth has allowed me to move fluidly between roles as a systems programmer, network supervisor, hardware engineer, consultant, and operations manager.

## Operational & Engineering Lineage

- **Crusan & Co. — Founder & Chief Systems Architect (`AxisOS™`)**:
  Architected the **AxisOS™** platform from first principles: a 100% RAM-resident operating system (`0.00%` flash wear, power-pull corruption immune), freestanding C99 userland utilities, deterministic `RAMGAK` memory bracketing, the **Sovereign Seven (`Sov-7`)** local AI daemon fleet, and bare-metal firmware for Raspberry Pi and RP2350 / ESP32-S3 micro-nodes (`Cub`). Author of ***The Pocket Professor's Guide to AxisOS™*** technical book series.
- **Legislative Affairs Agency (State of Alaska) — Network & Programming Operations Lead**:
  Led network and software engineering operations across three data centers, supervising both hardware technicians and application developers while maintaining uptime, security, and code quality across one of the most geographically dispersed government infrastructures in North America.
- **Macro\*World Research (later Wachovia) — Senior Programmer/Analyst**:
  Architected and deployed high-reliability wireless and push-alert systems that dramatically improved throughput and consolidated the production server footprint from four machines down to one.
- **Red Hat Software, IBM/CTG & Alaska ISP Operations**:
  Supported Red Hat Software as a Linux systems technician, developed Trilogy-based enterprise systems for IBM/CTG, and founded and operated my own computer services company and seven-city ISP in Alaska, building custom hardware and regional network infrastructure from the ground up.
- **United States Army (`1986–1998`) — Staff Sergeant (`11B3G`)**:
  Served twelve years in the U.S. Army, rising to Staff Sergeant. Contributed to the automation of the **United Nations Command Security Force (UNCSF)**, supported the **War Fighter** project, secured classified communications systems, trained soldiers, and maintained mission-critical field hardware. Every system we build at **Crusan & Co.** carries that NCO standard forward: know what your hardware is doing, verify ground truth, and never leave a single point of failure unguarded.
